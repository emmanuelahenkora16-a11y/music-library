    How it works

    Find your song

    Search by title or composer, or pick a season and a song type.

    Open the score

    The PDF opens on this page. Tonic sol-fa and staff scores have different badges.

    Learn your part

    Press play on the audio and sing or play along.

    For music leaders

    Only a few music leaders add songs. To add one:

    • Upload the PDF and audio to the shared folder and set sharing to "Anyone with the link".
    • Add a row to the song sheet with the title, season, type, notation and links.
    • Check that the song shows up here and the score opens.
